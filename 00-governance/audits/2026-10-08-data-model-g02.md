## Freeze scope

`PASS_CORE_LOGICAL_MODEL` authorizes physical SQL design for the **technical CMMS core**:

- Asset references/context links;
- Maintenance Engineering plan structures;
- Triggering/Condition Monitoring core;
- WorkOrder core;
- Planning/Scheduling core;
- Execution Feedback core;
- governance/provenance references.

## Explicitly outside this freeze

- inventory/warehouse implementation;
- procurement/purchasing;
- contracts/subcontracts;
- invoicing/financial settlement;
- full Permit-to-Work/LOTO domain;
- predictive/AI models;
- failure-reporting/RCA detailed domain;
- definitive KPI catalog.

These future domains may extend the core through governed interfaces but must not invalidate current entity ownership.

## SQL design rules carried forward

1. Physical schema must preserve domain boundaries.
2. Published/versioned masters remain immutable.
3. WorkOrder does not own trigger calculations.
4. Schedule dates do not overwrite DueAt.
5. Planned resources and actual resources remain separate.
6. Historical effective revision references are retained.
7. No silent overwrite of authoritative execution timestamps.
8. Costs/financial closure remain separable from technical close.

`DM-G02 = PASS_CORE_LOGICAL_MODEL`.

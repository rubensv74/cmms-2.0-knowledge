## Why physical SQL is not yet authorized

A physical WorkOrder model would currently force unvalidated decisions about:

- preparation/release/start/complete/technical-close states;
- planner vs scheduler ownership;
- assignment/team/shift/capacity relations;
- replanning and due-date change semantics;
- actual labor/material/tool records;
- finding → corrective-work cardinality;
- which timestamps are authoritative for MTBF/MTTR and plan-vs-actual.

These are not cosmetic details; they determine table boundaries, history and integrity constraints.

## Gate conclusion

`DM-G02 = BLOCKED_BY_WORK_MANAGEMENT_CORE`.

No final CMMS SQL schema should be frozen yet.
